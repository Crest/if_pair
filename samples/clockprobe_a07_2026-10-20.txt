# Second run, same machine, with the interrupt-enabled scan (20 s) and the
# per-CPU extension lag.  Reading: the counter never steps back; the
# kernel's tc_cpu_ticks() does jump by a period, 77 times in 20 s on cpu 1
# while the probe called it 19 M times per second: statclock() reads the
# ticker from the clock interrupt (kern_clock.c, "new_switchtime =
# cpu_ticks()"), and tc_cpu_ticks() runs in a critical section with
# interrupts enabled, so an interrupt between a thread's read of the
# counter and its store of "last" leaves "last" newer than the thread's
# value, which the thread then takes for a 32-bit wrap.  Every such event
# adds 2^32 ticks (171.8 s) to that CPU's ticker for good.  Idle CPUs lose
# periods instead (a wrap that passes with no read on the CPU is not seen).
# cpu 1 and cpu 2 are the CPUs the kwq benchmarks have used for weeks.
