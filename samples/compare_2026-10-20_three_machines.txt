#   K3     5fac285  get_cyclecount() stamps, NET passes in wall time (2026-10-19)
#   K4off  685949e  the share limit present but off (cap_permille=1000)
#   K4     685949e  the share limit at its default, an equal share
# Tests (tests/compare.sh): tput1 = one flow, Gbit/s and the kernel's
# cp_times shares of the worker CPU W; tput16 = sixteen flows; udp_idle /
# udp_load = UDP request-reply RTT us (p50 / p99 / max) alone and beside the

# W's shares, the flow's Gbit/s); timer = 1 kHz timeshare timer with 100 us of
# work pinned to W beside the flow (lateness p99 / max, turnaround p99, us);
# exec = fork+exec every 20 ms pinned to W (avg / p99 us); machine = the
# timer unpinned under 2 x ncpu connections.  The flows are put on W by
# computing ports from each load's hash seed (victim ports).  Numbers in
# brackets: change against T.  Raw rows: compare_2026-10-20_{guest,a07,lab}.tsv.
